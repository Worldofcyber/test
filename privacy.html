Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 August 2026. This is a starter template — have a lawyer review it before you rely on it, especially for EU (GDPR) or California (CCPA) visitors.

What this site is

CyberWorld is an information-only publication. We publish articles about cybersecurity awareness. We do not offer accounts, logins, downloads, or any product or service that requires you to create a profile with us.

What we collect

Because this is a read-only information site, we collect very little directly from you:

  • Nothing, if you just read articles. Browsing the site does not require you to submit any personal information.
  • Standard server logs. Like virtually all websites, our hosting provider automatically logs technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, used only for security and basic traffic analysis.
  • Information you send us directly. If you email or call us using the details on our Contact page, we retain that correspondence to respond to you.

Advertising & cookies

This site displays ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance.

You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of participating third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Analytics

If analytics software (such as Google Analytics or a privacy-focused alternative) is added to this site, it will be disclosed here, along with any related cookie-consent requirements.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general adult audience and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact
