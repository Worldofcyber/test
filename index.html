THE PROCESS

Three stages, same as any kiln.

Stage 1

Capture

Paste a transcript, forward an email thread, or drop in a voice memo. Kiln takes whatever shape your notes are already in.

Stage 2

Shape

It finds the decisions, owners, and open questions buried in the mess, and arranges them into a structure that matches the doc type you need.

Stage 3

Fire

Out comes a finished document — headings, action items, and all — hardened enough to send, still editable if you want to reshape it.
